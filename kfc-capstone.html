Introduction &
Background

KFC entered Pakistan in 1997 with its first outlet in Gulshan Iqbal, Karachi. Today, over 120 outlets operate across the country. In late 2023, the Israel-Palestine conflict triggered a global boycott movement that placed KFC at the center of public backlash in Pakistan.

October 2023
Boycott Movement Begins
#BoycottKFC trends on Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok. Lists of "Israel-linked brands" circulate widely.
Late 2023
Sales Begin to Drop
Daily sales at KFC outlets fall sharply. Lucky One Mall Karachi drops from PKR 1.5M to PKR 800K average per day.
Early 2024
PSL Partnership Backlash
#BoycottPSL trends after KFC sponsors the cricket league. Stadium crowds visibly smaller; viewership declines.
March 2024
Mirpur Outlet Torched
Protesters burn a KFC outlet in Mirpur, AJK. Physical demonstrations spread across cities.
2024
Local Brands Emerge
Kababjees, Krispy 2 Go, OPTP Fried Chicken rapidly gain loyal consumer base as ethical local alternatives.

Literature
Review

Food Quality & Consistency
Meyer et al. (2015) — High-quality ingredients and consistent cooking standards are pivotal. Hassan et al. (2018) — Consistency builds trust and repeat visits.
Customer Satisfaction & Loyalty
Zeithaml et al. (2000) — Service quality affects satisfaction which influences retention. Parasuraman et al. (1988) — 5 dimensions: tangibles, reliability, responsiveness, assurance, empathy.
Location & Accessibility
Kotler et al. (2015) — Location is critical for foot traffic. Proximity to malls and transport hubs drives KFC's Pakistan expansion strategy.
Switching Intention
Kuk et al. (2010) — Dissatisfaction is the dominant trigger. Bansal et al. (2005) — Push (dissatisfaction), Pull (alternatives), Mooring (switching barriers) framework.
Boycott Intention
Shim et al. (2021) — Boycott is action to punish ethically irresponsible firms. Romani et al. (2013) — Affects brand image, product perception, and consumer confidence.
Alternative Attractiveness
Jones et al. (2000) — Perceived alternatives are a pull factor for switching. Alya E. Sjioen (2018) — Positive significant effect on switching intention.

Conclusion &
Recommendations

The boycott had a profound and measurable impact on KFC's customer retention in Pakistan. Over 74% expressed willingness to switch to local brands, while KFC outlets experienced a ~47% decline in daily sales. The movement permanently reshaped the Pakistani fast-food landscape.

"Cultural relevance, brand ethics, and community trust are critical for retention in Muslim-majority markets. KFC's slow and inadequate response aggravated the situation significantly."

— Research Conclusion
01
Menu Innovation
Launch new flavors, healthier options, and region-specific dishes that appeal to local Pakistani preferences and dietary habits.
02
Improve Customer Service
Train employees for outstanding service. Establish collaborative community relationships to rebuild trust with local consumers.
03
Transparent Communication
Use social media and brand ambassadors to communicate positively. Address ethical concerns with real, visible changes.
