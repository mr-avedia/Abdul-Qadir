Founder's
Message

Dear Team, Clients & Stakeholders,

It is with great pride that I present TechEmpire's Annual Report for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. What started as a small startup with a bold vision has grown into a trusted digital solutions partner. Our revenue reached PKR 842 million, a growth of 28.4% — driven by strong demand for our web development, e-commerce, WordPress, graphic design, and accounting software services.

This year we delivered 386 client projects, expanded our team, and deepened our expertise across all service lines. Our CEO Hanif Abdul Sattar and Co-Founder Shahzaib have been instrumental in scaling our operations, while our development team led by Haseeb and Yasir delivered exceptional quality across every project.

Looking ahead to FY 2025, we aim to grow our recurring client base, launch new accounting software features, and expand our e-commerce portfolio. I am deeply grateful to our entire team for their dedication and to our clients for their continued trust in TechEmpire.

Amin Noorani
Founder · TechEmpire Pvt. Ltd. · February 28, 2025

Notes to the
Financial Statements

Note 1 — Basis of Preparation

These financial statements are prepared in accordance with IFRS as adopted in Pakistan and the Companies Act, 2017. Presented in PKR. Prepared under the historical cost convention except for financial instruments at fair value.

Note 2 — Revenue Recognition (IFRS 15)

Time-and-material contracts: recognised over time based on hours incurred. Fixed-price projects: percentage-of-completion. SaaS subscriptions: rateably over subscription period. Consulting retainers: monthly as services rendered.

Note 3 — Depreciation Policy (IAS 16)

Straight-line basis: IT equipment & servers — 3 years · Furniture & fixtures — 7 years · Vehicles — 5 years · Leasehold improvements — over lease term. Intangible assets amortised over 3–5 years.

Note 4 — Related Party Transactions

Key management remuneration: PKR 28.4M (FY 2023: PKR 22.1M). All related party transactions conducted on arm's length terms and approved by the Board. No other material related party transactions during the year.

Note 5 — Contingencies & Commitments

Outstanding capital commitments: PKR 8.2M for server infrastructure (Q1 2025). Operating lease commitments: PKR 34.4M within one year, PKR 62.8M thereafter. No material legal proceedings as at December 31, 2024.
