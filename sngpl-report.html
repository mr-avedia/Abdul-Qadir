Section 01

Company
Introduction

Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited (SNGPL), established in 1963 as a private limited company, became a public limited company in January 1964 under the Indian Companies Act 1913. SNGPL is the largest gas company in northern Pakistan, serving over 7.2 million consumers across Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Azad Jammu & Kashmir.

SNGPL supplies gas through two sources: System Gas — extracting 750–800 million cubic feet from local sources — and RLNG (Re-gasified Liquified Natural Gas) imported in liquid form, totaling approximately 100 million cubic feet.

Vision

To be the leading integrated natural gas provider, improving quality of life and achieving maximum benefit for stakeholders through uninterrupted, environment-friendly energy.

Mission

Deliver natural gas to all doorsteps through continuous expansion of the network, optimally employing technological, human, and organizational resources with high ethical standards.

Section 05

Behavioral Biases
Identified

Cognitive Biases
Overreaction Bias
Investors overreact to announcements — sharp price spikes followed by corrections. Seen on July 5, Aug 27, Nov 22.
Anchoring Bias
Past dividend of PKR 7 anchored expectations; PKR 4 announcement caused disappointment and sell-off.
Confirmation Bias
Investors focused on dividend news while ignoring auditor's investigation allegation on July 29, 2021.
Social Biases
Herding Behavior
Trade volume jumped from 2.1M to 21.4M on July 5 as investors followed early buyers without independent analysis.
Overconfidence Bias
After AGM notice (July 7) and court win (July 19), investors overestimated ability to predict outcomes.
Status Quo Bias
Repeated AGM extension requests led investors to hold positions and wait, evidenced by reduced trade volume.
Emotional Biases
Loss Aversion
Investors sold on declining prices to avoid greater losses — even at a loss — during Aug–Oct 2021 downtrend.
Recency Bias
Most recent announcements (dividend, quarterly EPS) weighted heavily over longer historical performance.
Regret Aversion
Before June 2022 board meeting, investors avoided selling fearing the price would recover after they sold.

Section 08

Investment
Strategies

01
Portfolio Diversification
Invest across different asset classes (bonds, equities, commodities) and sectors (technology, healthcare, energy). SNGPL's share fell from Rs. 48.34 to Rs. 34.21 in one fiscal year — concentrated risk causes severe losses. Diversification minimizes sector-specific impact.
02
Value Investing
SNGPL shares became undervalued due to continuous AGM/report submission delays. However, company remained profitable throughout. High chance of price bouncing back to intrinsic value. Breakup value (PKR 62.51) significantly exceeds market price (PKR 34.21) — a clear value signal.
03
Set Loss Limits (Stop-Loss)
Set predetermined criteria for cutting losses in underperforming shares. This prevents emotional decisions driven by anchoring bias (holding on to previous high prices) or loss aversion (waiting too long and incurring larger losses).
04
Strategic Asset Allocation
Align investment portfolio with personal risk tolerance and investment horizon. Adjust asset mix as financial goals or market conditions change. Long-term perspective helps mitigate short-term fluctuations and recency bias driven by daily news cycle.
Section 09

Risk
Analysis

Market Risk
High
Pakistan's high inflation rate and volatile energy sector — influenced by international events — means even well-diversified portfolios face broad market decline risk. SNGPL lost ~29% market value in one year.
Liquidity Risk
Medium
During negative news cycles (AGM extensions), finding buyers without accepting lower prices becomes difficult. Ongoing submission delays increased liquidity risk significantly for existing shareholders.
Behavioral Risk
Medium
Investors are neither fully rational nor irrational — psychological biases lead to suboptimal decisions. Herding, overreaction, and loss aversion caused price distortions not justified by fundamentals.
Mitigation 01 — Regular Review
Review investment portfolio periodically to ensure alignment with risk tolerance and investment goals. Rebalance when required to maintain desired asset allocation.
Mitigation 02 — Continuous Learning
Stay informed about market conditions and behavioral finance concepts. Understanding investor biases helps in recognizing and avoiding them in personal decision-making.
Mitigation 03 — Professional Advisory
Engage with a qualified financial advisor for portfolio advice and guidance during volatile periods. Professional input helps in sticking to long-term strategy and avoiding emotional decisions.
